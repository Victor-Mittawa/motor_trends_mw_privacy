User Data Deletion Instructions

Motor Trends MW respects your privacy and gives users the ability to request deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

How to Request Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your Motor Trends MW account and associated personal information, follow these steps:

  1. Open your email application.
  2. Send an email to our official support address.
  3. Use the subject: Motor Trends MW Account Deletion Request.
  4. Include your registered email address and the name associated with your account.
  5. If you registered using Facebook, mention that you used Facebook Login. If you registered using Google, mention Google Login.
Request Account Deletion
Important: Sending a deletion request does not automatically delete your account. Our team will review and verify your request before processing it.

What Information Will Be Deleted?

Following verification, we will arrange deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate retention obligations.

This may include:

  • Your Motor Trends MW account and profile information.
  • Your stored authentication account information.
  • Personal information associated with your account.
  • Other account-linked information that we are permitted to delete.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes. Public contributions and shared content may also require separate handling.

Facebook Login Users

If you used Facebook to sign in to Motor Trends MW, you can also remove the application's connection to your Facebook account.

Open your Facebook settings, navigate to Apps and Websites, locate Motor Trends MW and choose Remove.

Removing the Facebook connection prevents the continued use of that connection, but does not automatically delete information already stored in your Motor Trends MW account. You should still submit a deletion request to us.

How Long Does Deletion Take?

After receiving your request, our team will review it, verify account ownership and communicate the expected processing timeline.

Information retained in backups may remain temporarily until it is removed through our normal backup retention processes.
