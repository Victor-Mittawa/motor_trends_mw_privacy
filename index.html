Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 25 November 2026

Motor Trends MW ("MTMW", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored, and the choices available to you when using Motor Trends MW.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, profile picture and authentication information when you register or sign in.
  • Social login information: Information made available to us by Google or Facebook when you choose either service to authenticate your account.
  • User-generated content: Posts, comments, messages, photographs, videos and other information you voluntarily submit through the app.
  • Device and technical information: Device details, application version, diagnostic information and technical logs where collected.
  • Usage information: Information about how you interact with application features, where collected.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate users through Google and Facebook.
  • Display your profile and community contributions.
  • Enable posting, commenting and communication features.
  • Store and display uploaded photographs and videos.
  • Maintain, improve and secure the application.
  • Respond to support requests and user enquiries.

3. Third-Party Services

Motor Trends MW uses third-party services to support authentication, data storage and application functionality. These providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

  • Google Firebase: Used for services including authentication and application data storage. Firebase Privacy Information .
  • Cloudinary: Used to store and deliver media such as photographs and videos. Cloudinary Privacy Policy .
  • Facebook: Used as an optional authentication provider when you choose Facebook login. Facebook Privacy Policy .
  • Google: Used as an optional authentication provider when you choose Google login. Google Privacy Policy .

4. Data Storage and Security

We use third-party infrastructure providers to store and process information required to operate the application. We take reasonable measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers when necessary to operate the application, comply with applicable legal obligations, protect users or address security issues.

Content that you choose to publish publicly within the community may be visible to other users. Please consider this before sharing personal information.

6. User Account and Data Deletion

You may request the deletion of your Motor Trends MW account and associated personal information by contacting us using the email address below.

Please include your registered email address and clearly state that you wish to delete your account. We may request reasonable information to verify ownership of the account.

We will process verified requests within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal obligations and any information we are required to retain. Some information may remain in backups for a limited period before being removed through normal retention processes.

7. Children's Privacy

Motor Trends MW is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information without the required authorization, please contact us.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You may choose whether to use Google or Facebook authentication. You may also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application, services or legal requirements change. Any updates will be published on this page together with a revised effective date.
